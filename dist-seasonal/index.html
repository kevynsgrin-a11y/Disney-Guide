Festival

EPCOT International Flower & Garden Festival

The best food value of the four EPCOT festivals and the worst calendar — go in the opening fortnight before spring break arrives, and it is comfortably the strongest of the four for families with children.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate February/early March – early July
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Festival

EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival

The best festival Disney runs and the most expensive way to eat at a theme park — go on a weekday, budget around $60 a head, and treat the booths as dinner rather than as snacks you graze between rides.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate July/August – mid-November
  • TicketIncluded with admission

After hours

Disney After Hours

The best per-ride value Walt Disney World sells, and only if you ride hard for the full three hours — buy it at Hollywood Studios or Magic Kingdom, and do not buy it at Animal Kingdom.

Last confirmed cycle

  • Runsno fixed opening — nights are released in batches through the year – no fixed close — the calendar has historically run heaviest from mid-winter to late summer
  • Typical price$139–$209 / night

After hours

Disneyland After Dark

A series of separately ticketed themed nights rather than one recurring event, and the theme is what you are actually paying for - the short ride waits are a side effect, not the product. Buy it when the night's theme is something you would pay for on its own, and never buy it as a way to skip lines.

Last confirmed cycle

  • Runslate January – late June
  • Typical price$139–$209 / night

Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party

Worth buying once, on an early-season weeknight, and only if you accept that you are paying for a parade, a fireworks show and character lines that exist on no other date — not for a night of short ride waits.

Expected — not yet announced

  • Runsmid-August – 1 November
  • Typical price$119–$219 / night

Separate ticket

Oogie Boogie Bash - A Disney Halloween Party

The best hard-ticket night either Disney resort runs, and the hardest one to buy: Oogie Boogie Bash sells out months before the season starts, which makes the ticket-buying strategy more important than anything that happens at the party itself.

Expected — not yet announced

  • Runsearly to mid-August – 31 October
  • Typical price$154–$224 / night

Included overlay

Halloween Time at the Disneyland Resort

Included with any regular ticket, which is the whole argument for it, and the two attractions it repaints are better than the versions they replace. What you give up is those versions: Haunted Mansion Holiday is what you get instead of the Haunted Mansion, not in addition to it, and it holds the longest wait in its park all season.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate August – 31 October
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Why this site says "expected" so often

Search for any Disney event and you will find a dozen pages stating this year's dates with total confidence. Check the operator's own site and a good share of them turn out to be last year's dates, or a guess dressed as a fact.

We publish the pattern — which is stable, useful, and true — and we mark the dates as confirmed only once somebody has actually announced them. A page that has drifted past its own review date says so in a banner nobody here can switch off.

It costs us rich results on about half the event pages for part of each year. We think being right is the more durable asset.

The permanent version of all this

Height requirements, ride-by-ride detail, accessibility, permanent dining and park maps live on Ride Ready Guide, our year-round sister site. Those facts do not move with the season, so we link rather than repeat — one canonical owner per topic, in both directions.
