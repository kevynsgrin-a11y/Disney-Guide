Holidays

Disney on New Year's Eve: what is running, what it costs, and whether to go at all

Neither resort sells a New Year's Eve party, both hit their annual capacity ceiling on the night itself, and the honest verdict is that this is the worst-value date on the Disney calendar unless the date itself is the reason you are going.

The structure described here — no hard-ticket event, top-tier pricing, phased closure risk at Magic Kingdom, and the Disneyland Resort overlay running into January — has held across the 2023, 2024 and 2025 seasons. Your options, by resort

Both coasts do this differently, and the right answer depends on which one you are going to.

Walt Disney World

There is no New Year's Eve hard-ticket event at Walt Disney World. The Festival of the Holidays is listed here because people plan around it and then find it has closed — it typically ends the day before New Year's Eve rather than running through it. Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party finishes before Christmas and Disney Jollywood Nights ends in late December, so neither is available. What remains is a top-tier ticket, extended hours, a dedicated fireworks show at more than one park, and a real risk of phased closure at Magic Kingdom.
  • EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays — Go in the two weeks before Thanksgiving and treat the Candlelight Processional as the reason you came — go during Christmas week and you will pay the year's peak prices to stand still on a promenade that does not move.

Disneyland Resort

The Disneyland Resort sells no New Year's Eve party either, but its holiday overlay runs into early or mid-January and is included with any ticket. That single scheduling difference is why Anaheim is the better coast for anyone whose dates land in this window: arriving on New Year's Day rather than the day before New Year's Eve gets you the same ride overlays, the same parade and the same seasonal show at a materially lower crowd level and a lower ticket tier.
  • Holidays at the Disneyland Resort — The strongest value proposition of any seasonal offering at either Disney resort, because it is the only one on this scale that costs nothing beyond a regular ticket: five ride overlays, a parade, two nighttime shows, and a full food festival, all included. The price is paid in crowds, and in the last two weeks of December you pay a great deal of them.

The short answer

Do not plan a Disney trip around New Year's Eve, and if the date is fixed for you, spend the evening somewhere other than the park with the fireworks. There is no hard-ticket New Year's event at either resort. The seasonal evenings you may have read about — Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party, Disney Jollywood Nights — have all finished by then, and the EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays typically ends the day before New Year's Eve rather than running through it.

What you get on New Year's Eve is a normal park day at the top ticket tier, with an extended closing time, a special fireworks show at more than one park, and every park at or near capacity. Magic Kingdom has reached phased closure on the last two days of December in multiple recent years, which means guests without a park reservation or already inside are turned away.

The one genuinely good version of this trip exists at the Disneyland Resort. Holidays at the Disneyland Resort runs into early or mid-January, so the overlays, the parade and the seasonal show are all still up on New Year's Eve and for a week afterwards. Arrive on New Year's Day rather than the day before New Year's Eve and you get the same season for a fraction of the crowd.

What it costs, and why it costs that

New Year's Eve sits in the top pricing tier of every product both resorts sell. A one-day Walt Disney World ticket sat at the top of its $119 to $209 band as of July 2026, and a one-day Disneyland Resort ticket at the top of its $104 to $214 band. Lightning Lane Multi Pass prices at the top of its $15 to $45 Florida band and its $30 to $45 Anaheim band. Hotel rates at both resorts reach their annual ceiling in this week.

The reason is simple and worth stating plainly: demand on this date exceeds supply by a wider margin than on any other date of the year, and every one of Disney's prices is now demand-linked. There is no discount, no promotion and no residency offer that applies to New Year's Eve at either resort. Annual pass tiers below the top are blocked out.

A family of four doing one Florida park day on New Year's Eve with Lightning Lane is looking at roughly $700 to $1,000 before food, parking or a hotel. The same family on a mid-January Tuesday, with the Disneyland Resort holiday overlay still running, would spend roughly half that and see more.

What actually happens on the night

Both resorts extend park hours and run a dedicated fireworks show, usually at more than one park so the crowd splits. Walt Disney World has historically run a New Year's Eve show at Magic Kingdom and EPCOT with a repeat at midnight, and Hollywood Studios has run its own version. The Disneyland Resort runs a New Year's Eve show at Disneyland Park with a separate offering at Disney California Adventure.

The practical shape of the evening is that viewing space fills three to five hours before the show, park entry closes to new arrivals well before dark on the busiest years, and transport out afterwards is the worst of the year. Parking trams and monorails at Walt Disney World, and the Anaheim structures, clear crowds for well over an hour after closing.

There is no separate ticket to buy that improves this. Some Disney hotels and restaurants sell New Year's Eve dining events with a view, and those are the only paid upgrades that exist. They price well above their normal equivalents and sell out months ahead.

The plan if the date is not negotiable

Pick a park and stay in it. Park hopping on New Year's Eve is a way to spend two hours in transport queues and arrive at a second park that has already closed to new entry. Choose the park whose fireworks you want and commit to it by early afternoon.

Arrive at opening, not for the evening. The first two hours of a New Year's Eve park day are the only hours that behave normally. Standby waits at midday and in the evening at Magic Kingdom regularly exceed the annual average by a wide margin, and Lightning Lane inventory sells out early on this date at both resorts.

Eat before six. Every quick-service location and every walk-up list is saturated from late afternoon onward, and mobile order windows on this date can run more than an hour out. Book a table-service reservation for a late lunch rather than a dinner, or eat outside the parks.

Consider the alternative dates seriously. The day before New Year's Eve is nearly as busy and New Year's Day is materially quieter at both resorts, with the Disneyland Resort holiday overlay still running for another week or more. If seeing the season is the goal rather than the countdown, the first week of January is the answer.

Where the seasonal content actually still is

Holidays at the Disneyland Resort is the only major seasonal product that runs through New Year's Eve and beyond at either resort. The overlays, the Disneyland Park holiday parade and the seasonal nighttime show typically continue into early or mid-January, which makes Anaheim the one place where you can arrive after the peak and still get the whole season.

The EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays typically closes the day before New Year's Eve. That timing catches people out every year: a trip booked for the last days of December on the strength of the festival can arrive to find the kitchens closed and the World Showcase back to its ordinary line-up. Check the festival's own end date before you build a Florida trip around it.

Walt Disney World strips its holiday decorations in the first days of January. If the decorations are the reason for the trip, Florida rewards going early in the season and California rewards going late.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a New Year's Eve party ticket at either resort?

No. Neither Walt Disney World nor the Disneyland Resort sells a hard-ticket New Year's Eve event. The seasonal evenings that do exist — Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party, Disney Jollywood Nights, Oogie Boogie Bash — have all finished by then. The only paid upgrades are hotel and restaurant dining events with a fireworks view, which price well above their normal equivalents.

Will the parks close because they are full?

It is a genuine risk, and it has happened at Magic Kingdom on the last two days of December in multiple recent years. Disney operates phased closure, which restricts entry to guests already inside or with specific entitlements. If you are travelling on this date, be inside your park by early afternoon and do not plan to hop.

Is the EPCOT Festival of the Holidays running on New Year's Eve?

Usually not. The festival has typically ended the day before New Year's Eve rather than running through it, which catches out people who book the last week of December on the strength of it. Confirm the festival's own end date on the operator's calendar before you build a Florida trip around it.

What is the cheapest way to see the holiday season without the New Year crowds?

Two windows. The first two weeks of November at either resort, when everything is installed and prices sit near their annual floor. Or the first ten days of January at the Disneyland Resort only, where the overlay runs into early or mid-January after the crowds have gone. Walt Disney World strips its decorations in the first days of January, so the second option does not exist in Florida.

Do annual passes work on New Year's Eve?

Only the top tiers. Every lower pass tier at both resorts has historically been blocked out across the Christmas and New Year period, and pass blockouts are the point at which people discover that the cheap tier they bought does not cover the week they wanted. Check the blockout calendar for your specific dates before you count a pass as covering this trip.

Are any dates or prices on this page confirmed for 2026 and 2027?

No. The pattern described here is drawn from the 2023, 2024 and 2025 seasons and the prices are labelled with the cycle they came from. We have not verified a 2026 or 2027 New Year's operating calendar against either operator as of July 2026, and we will not publish one until we can.
