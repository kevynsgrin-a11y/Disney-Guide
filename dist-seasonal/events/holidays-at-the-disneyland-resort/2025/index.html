2025 dates are not announced

We will not print dates nobody has published. The pattern is on the main event page; when the operator announces, this page gets the real dates and a confirmed ribbon.

What is different in 2025

  • The 2025 into 2026 season ran the established roster: the small world, Haunted Mansion, Jungle Cruise, and two Cars Land overlays, with the parade, the seasonal fireworks, and the holiday Paradise Bay show.
  • Festival of Holidays ran again at Disney California Adventure alongside Viva Navidad in Paradise Gardens Park.
  • The holiday installation remained in place into January 2026, as it has in every recent season.
