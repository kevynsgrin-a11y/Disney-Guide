Park Season Guide

Every seasonal event at the US Disney parks

Parties, festivals, overlays and after-hours events at both resorts — what each one is, what it costs, and whether it is worth the money. Every page states whether this year is confirmed or still expected.

Walt Disney World

After hours

Disney After Hours

The best per-ride value Walt Disney World sells, and only if you ride hard for the full three hours — buy it at Hollywood Studios or Magic Kingdom, and do not buy it at Animal Kingdom.

Last confirmed cycle

  • Runsno fixed opening — nights are released in batches through the year – no fixed close — the calendar has historically run heaviest from mid-winter to late summer
  • Typical price$139–$209 / night

Separate ticket

Disney Jollywood Nights

The best ride value of Walt Disney World's holiday hard tickets and the weakest content value — a late-night party at Hollywood Studios that is really an after-hours ticket with a jazz band attached.

Last confirmed cycle

  • Runsmid-November – late December
  • Typical price$159–$209 / night

Festival

EPCOT International Festival of the Arts

The best EPCOT festival to build a whole park day around and the weakest one to eat at — come for the Broadway concert series, the working artists and the lowest crowds of the Walt Disney World year.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runsmid-January – late February
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Festival

EPCOT International Festival of the Holidays

Go in the two weeks before Thanksgiving and treat the Candlelight Processional as the reason you came — go during Christmas week and you will pay the year's peak prices to stand still on a promenade that does not move.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runsmid-November – 30 December
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Festival

EPCOT International Flower & Garden Festival

The best food value of the four EPCOT festivals and the worst calendar — go in the opening fortnight before spring break arrives, and it is comfortably the strongest of the four for families with children.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate February/early March – early July
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Festival

EPCOT International Food & Wine Festival

The best festival Disney runs and the most expensive way to eat at a theme park — go on a weekday, budget around $60 a head, and treat the booths as dinner rather than as snacks you graze between rides.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate July/August – mid-November
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Separate ticket

Mickey's Not-So-Scary Halloween Party

Worth buying once, on an early-season weeknight, and only if you accept that you are paying for a parade, a fireworks show and character lines that exist on no other date — not for a night of short ride waits.

Expected — not yet announced

  • Runsmid-August – 1 November
  • Typical price$119–$219 / night

Separate ticket

Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party

The weakest value of the three Walt Disney World hard-ticket parties, because almost everything people buy it for — the decorations, the castle lighting, the holiday ride overlays — is already included with an ordinary November park ticket.

Expected — not yet announced

  • Runsearly November – shortly before Christmas
  • Typical price$169–$219 / night

Disneyland Resort

Festival

Disney California Adventure Food & Wine Festival

A two-month food festival included with admission, and the whole value sits in its first three weeks, before the spring break crowd arrives - that stretch is the second of the two quietest good windows at either Disney resort. After the schools break, it is the same festival in a park twice as full at a higher ticket price.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate February or early March – late April
  • TicketIncluded with admission

After hours

Disneyland After Dark

A series of separately ticketed themed nights rather than one recurring event, and the theme is what you are actually paying for - the short ride waits are a side effect, not the product. Buy it when the night's theme is something you would pay for on its own, and never buy it as a way to skip lines.

Last confirmed cycle

  • Runslate January – late June
  • Typical price$139–$209 / night

Included overlay

Halloween Time at the Disneyland Resort

Included with any regular ticket, which is the whole argument for it, and the two attractions it repaints are better than the versions they replace. What you give up is those versions: Haunted Mansion Holiday is what you get instead of the Haunted Mansion, not in addition to it, and it holds the longest wait in its park all season.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runslate August – 31 October
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Included overlay

Holidays at the Disneyland Resort

The strongest value proposition of any seasonal offering at either Disney resort, because it is the only one on this scale that costs nothing beyond a regular ticket: five ride overlays, a parade, two nighttime shows, and a full food festival, all included. The price is paid in crowds, and in the last two weeks of December you pay a great deal of them.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runsmid-November – early to mid-January
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Festival

Lunar New Year at Disney California Adventure

Along with the spring Food & Wine Festival, this is one of the two quietest good windows at either Disney resort: a real food festival included with admission, running through the deadest stretch of the Disneyland Resort calendar, with headliner waits at their annual low and a genuine chance of walking on to Radiator Springs Racers before noon.

Expected — not yet announcedNo extra ticket

  • Runsmid-January – mid-February
  • TicketIncluded with admission

Separate ticket

Oogie Boogie Bash - A Disney Halloween Party

The best hard-ticket night either Disney resort runs, and the hardest one to buy: Oogie Boogie Bash sells out months before the season starts, which makes the ticket-buying strategy more important than anything that happens at the party itself.

Expected — not yet announced

  • Runsearly to mid-August – 31 October
  • Typical price$154–$224 / night

How to read the confidence labels

LabelWhat it meansWhat we will state
ConfirmedOfficially announced, with the announcement named on the page.Exact dates and prices.
ExpectedNot announced, but the pattern has held for at least three years.Windows and ranges. Never an exact date.
Last confirmed cycleThis cycle is unannounced or unclear.Last cycle's figures, labeled with their year.

Most competing pages state this year's dates whether or not anyone announced them. That is the practice this labelling exists to avoid.
