What a Disney parks trip costs right now

Tickets, Lightning Lane, parking, dining plans and annual passes at both resorts. Every figure is a range with the cycle it was checked against, because a single number is wrong for almost everyone.

Prices

Disney annual pass prices, and exactly how many days each tier takes to pay for itself

Every annual pass tier at both resorts pays for itself somewhere between two and eighteen park days, and the tier you should buy is decided almost entirely by blockout dates rather than by price — a cheap pass blocked on the only week you can travel is worth nothing at all.

Last confirmed cycle

Prices

Disney Dining Plan prices, and the arithmetic that decides whether to buy one

Neither Walt Disney World dining plan saves most families money at menu prices, and the single variable that decides it is alcohol — both plans include it for guests 21 and over, and without it the Quick-Service plan loses money for almost everyone who buys it.

Last confirmed cycle

Prices

Lightning Lane prices at both resorts, and what each one works out at per skipped queue

Lightning Lane costs between $15 and $449 per person per day depending on which product you buy and which date you buy it for, and the spread inside a single product is wide enough that moving your park day is worth more than any booking tactic.

Last confirmed cycle

Prices

Disney park ticket prices at both resorts, and the only number worth comparing

A Disney ticket's headline price is close to meaningless because the per-day cost falls by roughly half between a one-day ticket and a ten-day one, which makes trip length, not date-shopping, the decision that controls what you spend on admission.

Last confirmed cycle

Prices

Disney parking and transport costs at both resorts, and when to leave the car behind

Parking is the one Disney cost that is trivial to eliminate at Walt Disney World and awkward to eliminate at the Disneyland Resort, which reverses the usual advice about hiring a car — in Florida the free transport network is genuinely comprehensive, and in Anaheim a car is often the cheaper option.

Last confirmed cycle
