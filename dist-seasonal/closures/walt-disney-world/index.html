Closures

What is closed at Walt Disney World

Nine attractions across three Walt Disney World parks are either permanently closed or under construction as of July 2026, all of them in Frontierland, Grand Avenue and the former DinoLand, and none of them has a reopening date we are prepared to print.

Expected — not yet announced · Checked July 2026

Every item on this tracker is cross-checked against Ride Ready Guide's attraction dataset, which carries the status field reported here, and nothing appears on this page that our sister site does not carry as closed or under construction. Reopening timings are our reading of what Disney has said publicly; none is presented as an announced date, and the one item with no verifiable year is labelled as unannounced rather than estimated. Routine short-term refurbishments are not tracked.

Next review October 2026

Refurbishment schedules move constantly

Operators add and drop closures with little notice, and a reopening date is an intention rather than a commitment until the ride is actually running. Check the official app on the morning of your visit — this page is for planning, not for the day itself.

Rivers of AmericaMagic KingdomPermanently closedNot coming backDrained permanently as part of Magic Kingdom's western expansion work. Nothing returns to this waterway, and the two attractions that used it are closed with it. If you are planning around a Magic Kingdom map more than a year old, Frontierland and Liberty Square are the areas that have changed.
Liberty Belle RiverboatMagic KingdomPermanently closedNot coming backClosed with the Rivers of America. This was one of the few genuine sit-down rest attractions at Magic Kingdom, and its loss changes how a long day with small children or limited mobility should be paced.
Tom Sawyer IslandMagic KingdomPermanently closedNot coming backClosed with the Rivers of America that reached it. Magic Kingdom now has no free-roaming outdoor play area, which is worth knowing if you were counting on it as the afternoon release valve for a five-year-old.
Muppet*Vision 3DDisney's Hollywood StudiosPermanently closedNot coming backClosed permanently to clear the Grand Avenue area for redevelopment. Hollywood Studios has lost an air-conditioned seated show in a park that is short of them, which matters most between June and September.
DINOSAURDisney's Animal KingdomPermanently closedNot coming backClosed permanently for the Tropical Americas redevelopment of the former DinoLand. Its replacement is the Indiana Jones attraction listed below, in the same building footprint.
It's Tough to Be a BugDisney's Animal KingdomPermanently closedNot coming backThe show is gone; the theater beneath the Tree of Life is not. Ride Ready Guide carries the Tree of Life Theater as open with different programming, so treat this as a change of show rather than a lost venue.
Indiana Jones Adventure (Tropical Americas)Disney's Animal KingdomUnder construction2027The headline attraction of the Tropical Americas land replacing DinoLand. Disney has spoken about a 2027 opening publicly; we have not verified a date and are not going to print one. Treat 2027 as the earliest a trip could be planned around it, not as a commitment.
Pueblo Esperanza Carousel (Tropical Americas)Disney's Animal KingdomUnder constructionwith the Tropical Americas landA family spinner for the new land, expected to open alongside it rather than separately. Animal Kingdom has been short of young-child attractions since the DinoLand closures and this is the replacement for that gap.
Encanto Ride-Through (Tropical Americas)Disney's Animal KingdomUnder constructionafter the Indiana Jones attraction, no year givenAnnounced as part of Tropical Americas but sequenced behind the Indiana Jones attraction. No opening year has been given that we can verify, so this is the one item on the tracker we would not plan any trip around.
