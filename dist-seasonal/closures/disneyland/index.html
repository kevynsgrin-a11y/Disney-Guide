Closures

What is closed at Disneyland Resort

Six attractions across the two Anaheim parks are closed or under construction as of July 2026, and the one that will actually change your day is the Disneyland Monorail, which our sister site carries as closed with no return date we can verify.

Expected — not yet announced · Checked July 2026

Every item on this tracker is cross-checked against Ride Ready Guide's attraction dataset, which carries the status field reported here, and nothing appears that our sister site does not carry as closed or under construction. No reopening date is published for any Anaheim item because we could not verify one for any of them, including the Avengers Campus expansion, which Disney has discussed publicly. Routine short-term refurbishments are not tracked.

Next review October 2026

Refurbishment schedules move constantly

Operators add and drop closures with little notice, and a reopening date is an intention rather than a commitment until the ride is actually running. Check the official app on the morning of your visit — this page is for planning, not for the day itself.
