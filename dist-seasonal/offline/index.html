Independent, unofficial help with timing a Disney park trip: when to go, what is running, and what it costs.

Seasonal facts decay, so we date them. Every dated claim here carries the month a person last verified it, whether the operator has actually announced it or we are only expecting it, and the month it must be rechecked. Once that review month passes, the page says so automatically rather than waiting for us to remember. Confirm anything trip-critical with the operator before you book.
