December at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for December is C minus. Book the first ten days and December is excellent. Book the Christmas fortnight and you get the year's worst queues at the year's worst prices, with parks reaching capacity on several days.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. December runs peak. December has the sharpest internal split of any month on the Universal calendar, and the boundary is the day school lets out rather than any date on the calendar. The first ten days are among the quietest of the year at both resorts: no district is on break, the holiday overlays are fully installed, and Universal Orlando's midweek waits sit close to their September and early-November levels.

The stretch to avoid: the days between Christmas and the first days of January, the busiest of the Universal year.

If you have flexibility: the first ten days, the best-value window in the second half of the year.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 73 degrees, lows around 53, about 6 days with measurable rain. Mild and dry: low 70s afternoons, evenings in the mid-50s, and six rain days. Cold fronts can drop a day into the 50s with a wind, which is the condition that closes the Islands of Adventure water rides.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 67 degrees, lows around 45, about 5 days with measurable rain. The coolest month of the Hollywood year and the start of the wet season, with afternoons in the high 60s and evenings in the mid-40s. The Studio Tour tram is open-sided and the escalators between the lots are exposed, so a cold evening here is felt more than the numbers suggest.

Cost. December sits in the peak band. The two halves of December sit at opposite ends of the Universal price range. The first ten days carry low-season Orlando hotel rates, lower-tier ticket dates and cheap Express Pass, while delivering exactly the same holiday overlays as the fortnight that costs three times as much.

One thing worth knowing. The first ten days are the whole point of this month. The holiday overlays at both resorts are fully installed by the start of December and do not change between then and the new year. The crowd and the price change enormously.

Who December suits. The first ten days, when the holiday overlays are up and the crowds are not.

Who it does not. The fortnight from just before Christmas into the new year, the busiest of the Universal year.
