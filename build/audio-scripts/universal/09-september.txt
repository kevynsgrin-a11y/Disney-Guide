September at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for September is A. Empty daytime parks, cheap hotels, and the year's best event running at early-season prices. The costs are Orlando heat that has barely broken and the peak of hurricane season.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. September runs moderate. September has the widest gap between daytime and evening attendance of any month in the year, and understanding that gap is most of what there is to know about it. Every US school district is back, the international summer wave has gone, and the daytime parks at both resorts run at the lowest level they reach between February and the following January.

The stretch to avoid: every Halloween Horror Nights evening at Universal Studios Florida and Universal Studios Hollywood.

If you have flexibility: weekday daytimes at Islands of Adventure and Epic Universe, the quietest of the second half of the year.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 90 degrees, lows around 74, about 14 days with measurable rain. Barely cooler than August: high 80s to low 90s afternoons, humidity still high, and fourteen days with measurable rain. September is also the statistical peak of the Atlantic hurricane season, which matters far more to a September trip than the daily forecast does.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 90 degrees, lows around 64, about 1 days with measurable rain. Still genuinely hot in the afternoon, and September produces some of the year's most extreme valley heat when an offshore wind pattern sets up. Dry throughout, with evenings falling into the mid-60s, so a Halloween Horror Nights night here is comfortable in a way the same night in Orlando is not.

Cost. September sits in the low band. September is the cheapest month at Universal Orlando outside the middle of January and the first week of December, and it is the only one of those three that comes with the year's biggest event attached. Hotel rates fall to their autumn floor, weekday ticket dates sit in the lower tiers, and daytime Express Pass is affordable on any non-event day.

One thing worth knowing. Buy the event on a weeknight and buy it early in the season. Halloween Horror Nights is priced by date at both resorts and the spread across a single season is larger than for anything else Universal sells. A September weeknight and a late-October Saturday deliver the same houses, the same scare zones and the same hours at very different prices, with very different queue lengths.

Who September suits. Halloween Horror Nights at either resort, on a weeknight, at the bottom of its price band.

Who it does not. Families with young children, because the dominant seasonal content is an adult horror event.
