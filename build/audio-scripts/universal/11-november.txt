November at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for November is B plus. The first two weeks are close to ideal in Orlando: low crowds, low rates, and the best weather of the year. Thanksgiving week undoes all of it, and the holiday layer arrives only in the second half.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. November runs moderate. November is three distinct stretches and the middle one is the reason to come. The first few days carry the last of the Halloween season at both resorts and behave like late October.

The stretch to avoid: the Thanksgiving weekend at both resorts, especially the Saturday.

If you have flexibility: the first two full weeks after the Halloween season ends, the quietest daytime parks of the autumn.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 79 degrees, lows around 59, about 6 days with measurable rain. The best park weather of the Orlando year alongside February and March: high 70s afternoons, comfortable evenings, low humidity, and six rain days. The afternoon thunderstorm pattern is over, the water rides are still comfortable in the first half of the month, and a full open-to-close day is realistic again.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 74 degrees, lows around 50, about 3 days with measurable rain. Mild afternoons in the mid-70s with evenings dropping into the low 50s, and the first rain of the winter season usually arriving late in the month. Comfortable walking weather for a park built across two levels, and cool enough after dark to want a jacket.

Cost. November sits in the moderate band. November is priced in three pieces that match its crowd shape. The first two weeks sit near the autumn floor at Universal Orlando, hotel rates low, weekday ticket dates in the lower tiers, and Express Pass affordable, and they are the best-value fortnight of the second half of the year now that the Halloween event premium has gone.

One thing worth knowing. The first two weeks are the quiet the rest of the year does not have. Once the Halloween season closes and before the holiday crowd arrives, Universal Orlando runs about a fortnight of genuinely low attendance in the best weather it produces. Nothing else on the calendar combines those two, because the other low-crowd windows come with January cold, February water-ride closures or September heat.

Who November suits. The first two weeks in Orlando, the best combination of weather and crowd on the calendar.

Who it does not. The Thanksgiving week and weekend, which price and queue at summer levels at both resorts.
