April at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for April is C plus. Avoid the fortnight bracketing Easter and April becomes a good month, with warm weather, moderate crowds and Orlando prices stepping back down. Inside that fortnight it is as bad as July.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. April runs high. April's crowd shape is decided by one date, and everything else is downstream of it. The fortnight bracketing Easter runs at genuine peak at both resorts: it is the last national break before summer, it draws heavily from the northeast and from Latin America into Orlando, and unlike March's staggered breaks it concentrates almost everybody into the same ten days.

The stretch to avoid: the week before and the week after Easter, at both resorts.

If you have flexibility: the last week to ten days, once every district is back at school.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 84 degrees, lows around 61, about 5 days with measurable rain. The driest month of the Orlando year and one of the two or three most pleasant, with mid-80s afternoons and comfortable mornings. Humidity starts to build in the last third of the month, which is the first hint of what June onward is like, and the water rides at Islands of Adventure become a reward rather than a risk.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 76 degrees, lows around 52, about 3 days with measurable rain. The wet season is effectively over and the summer heat has not arrived: mid-70s afternoons, cool evenings, and three rain days in the month. On the raw numbers this is the best park weather Universal Studios Hollywood gets all year.

Cost. April sits in the high band. April is priced as two months rather than one. The Easter fortnight carries top-tier ticket dates, peak Orlando hotel rates and the highest Express Pass prices of the first half of the year, and it sells out earlier than any other spring window because the dates are fixed and everybody knows them.

One thing worth knowing. Find Easter first, then plan everything else around it. Easter moves between late March and late April, and where it falls determines whether this is a good month or a bad one. Look it up before you look at anything else, because it is the only variable in April that is both knowable a year ahead and worth more than every tactic combined.

Who April suits. The last week or ten days of the month, which is the quiet before summer at both resorts.

Who it does not. The fortnight around Easter, which is peak in every measure at both resorts.
