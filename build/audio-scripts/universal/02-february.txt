February at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for February is A. Low crowds, low prices, the most comfortable Orlando weather of the year, and Mardi Gras running at no extra charge. Avoid the Presidents' week and this is the month we would book.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. February runs low. February is a low month with a hard spike in the middle of it. Outside the Presidents' Day week, Orlando runs at or near its annual low: no US district is on break, international travel is in its post-holiday trough, and the three parks absorb what attendance there is comfortably enough that midweek waits stay under twenty minutes on most non-headliners.

The stretch to avoid: the Presidents' Day long weekend and the school week attached to it, at both resorts.

If you have flexibility: the first full week, before any district breaks.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 74 degrees, lows around 52, about 7 days with measurable rain. This is the most comfortable park weather Orlando produces: mid-70s afternoons, low humidity by Florida standards, and enough of a chill in the morning to want a layer. The caveat is water rides.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 68 degrees, lows around 47, about 6 days with measurable rain. February is the second half of the Los Angeles wet season and behaves like January: mostly dry days, a handful of genuinely wet ones, and evenings in the high 40s. Afternoons in the high 60s are the most walkable conditions of the year for a park built on a hillside.

Cost. February sits in the low band. February outside the Presidents' week prices almost identically to mid-January and is the better month for the same money, which is what puts it at the top of this calendar. Orlando hotel rates hold near their annual floor, ticket dates sit in the lower tiers on weekdays, and Express Pass stays cheap enough on midweek dates that a family can buy it for a single park day without restructuring the budget.

One thing worth knowing. Mardi Gras is included, and most visitors do not realise it. Universal Orlando's Mardi Gras runs on regular park admission. The parade, the street party and the headline concerts are part of your ticket, and the only thing sold separately is the food and the reserved viewing packages.

Who February suits. First-time Universal Orlando trips that need three park days without the heat.

Who it does not. The Presidents' Day week, which prices and queues like a summer week.
