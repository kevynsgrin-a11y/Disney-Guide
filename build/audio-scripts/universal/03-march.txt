March at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for March is C. Excellent weather, heavy crowds, and no reliably quiet week to escape into. Go in March if the school calendar decides for you, and buy Express Pass rather than hoping for a gap.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. March runs high. March is busy for the whole month rather than in a spike, and that changes what planning effort is worth. US school districts stagger spring break across four or five weeks, so there is no single week that empties and no single week that is catastrophically worse than the others; whichever week you pick, some large state is off.

The stretch to avoid: the middle two weeks, when the largest state districts overlap.

If you have flexibility: the first few days, before most districts break.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 79 degrees, lows around 57, about 7 days with measurable rain. Close to ideal: high 70s afternoons, comfortable mornings, humidity still low enough that a full day outdoors is reasonable, and warm enough that the Islands of Adventure water rides stop being a mistake. Cold fronts still reach central Florida this month and can knock ten degrees off for a day or two.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 71 degrees, lows around 49, about 5 days with measurable rain. The tail of the wet season, with most days dry and the occasional soaking one. Low 70s afternoons make this the most comfortable month for the walking and the escalators a Universal Studios Hollywood day involves, and evenings still need a layer.

Cost. March sits in the high band. March prices like a shoulder-to-peak month at Universal Orlando and gets away with it because demand does not flinch. Hotel rates step up from the winter floor and stay there for the whole month, date-based ticket pricing sits in the upper-middle tiers on almost every date, and Express Pass, the most demand-sensitive thing Universal sells, runs at levels closer to July than to February on the busiest weeks.

One thing worth knowing. Stop looking for a quiet week and buy the day back instead. In February or November, moving your trip a week is the highest-value decision available. In March it is worth almost nothing, because the districts that make up the crowd rotate through the month rather than clustering.

Who March suits. Weather, which is the best Orlando produces all year alongside February and November.

Who it does not. Anyone trying to date-shop, because spring break staggers across the whole month.
