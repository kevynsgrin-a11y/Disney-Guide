October at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for October is B minus. The best weather and the best event of the Universal year, sold at the worst prices and the longest queues. Take a weeknight in the first half and October becomes a very good month.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. October runs high. October runs on a weekly cycle rather than a monthly one, and the cycle steepens as the month goes on. Weeknights early in the month are still close to September's level; the last three Fridays and Saturdays before Halloween are the busiest nights Universal Studios Florida and Universal Studios Hollywood see all year, including anything in July.

The stretch to avoid: the last three Fridays and Saturdays before Halloween, the busiest nights of the Universal year.

If you have flexibility: weeknights in the first half of the month, which still price and queue like September.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 85 degrees, lows around 67, about 9 days with measurable rain. The month the Florida summer finally breaks: mid-80s afternoons early on, falling into the low 80s by the end, humidity dropping noticeably, and nine rain days instead of the summer's seventeen. Evenings in the high 60s are what make a five-hour night event outdoors comfortable rather than an endurance test.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 83 degrees, lows around 58, about 2 days with measurable rain. Warm dry afternoons in the low 80s and evenings dropping into the high 50s, which is the single best night-event weather either resort produces. Early October can still deliver a heat spike well into the 90s when an offshore wind pattern sets up, so do not pack only for the average.

Cost. October sits in the high band. October is the most expensive month Universal runs outside July and the Christmas fortnight, and almost all of the increase is concentrated in the event. Halloween Horror Nights tickets on the last three weekends before Halloween sit at the top of their date-based range, event Express Pass reaches its annual ceiling and sells out in advance for the busiest nights, and Orlando hotel rates climb back from their September floor across the month.

One thing worth knowing. The date you pick is worth more than everything else combined. A Halloween Horror Nights ticket for a weeknight in the first half of October and one for the last Saturday before Halloween buy the same houses, the same scare zones and the same hours. They do not cost the same and they do not deliver the same number of houses, because the queues on a peak Saturday can run several times longer.

Who October suits. Halloween Horror Nights at full strength, with every house open and the season fully staffed.

Who it does not. Anyone travelling on the last three weekends, which are the peak of the Universal autumn.
