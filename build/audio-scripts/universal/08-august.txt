August at Universal Orlando and Universal Hollywood. Your month in about three minutes, from Hollywood Ride Guide.

Our grade for August is C minus. The last two weeks are one of the best-value windows Universal sells, and the first two are July. The whole month is hot, and Orlando adds hurricane risk to the deal.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. August runs high. August is the steepest crowd gradient on the Universal calendar and the two coasts run down it at different times. Universal Hollywood empties first: Southern California districts return in the middle of August, earlier than most of the country, so the park drops from summer level to genuinely moderate weekdays around mid-month while Orlando is still full.

The stretch to avoid: the first two weeks at Universal Orlando, indistinguishable from July.

If you have flexibility: the last full week at both resorts, the sharpest attendance drop of the year.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Universal Orlando: highs around 92 degrees, lows around 75, about 17 days with measurable rain. Functionally identical to July: low 90s afternoons, overnight lows that never drop below the mid-70s, and seventeen days with measurable rain. The difference is hurricane season, which reaches its most active stretch from mid-August, and which is a trip-planning risk rather than a daily-weather one.

Universal Hollywood: highs around 94 degrees, lows around 66, about 0 days with measurable rain. The hottest month of the Hollywood year, with afternoons in the mid-90s and occasional stretches well above that. It stays dry and it still cools into the mid-60s overnight, so the evening is comfortable in a way an Orlando August evening never is.

Cost. August sits in the moderate band. August is the fastest-falling price on the Universal calendar, and where you sit in the month decides whether it is expensive or cheap. The first two weeks carry summer hotel rates, upper-tier ticket dates and summer Express Pass pricing at Universal Orlando.

One thing worth knowing. Move your dates to the back half of the month and everything improves at once. The gap between the first week of August and the last week is the largest same-month swing in crowd and price that Universal Orlando produces. Hotel rates fall, ticket tiers fall, Express Pass falls, and daytime queues fall, all within about three weeks and all in the same direction.

Who August suits. The last two weeks in Orlando, when crowds fall, rates fall and Halloween Horror Nights has usually opened.

Who it does not. The first two weeks, which carry July's crowds and July's prices with none of the payoff.
