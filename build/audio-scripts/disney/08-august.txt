August at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for August is C. The first half is July with worse humidity. The second half is one of the year's genuine value windows at Walt Disney World and still peak season at Disneyland.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. August runs moderate. August's split is driven by school calendars rather than by anything Disney does, and it is one of the sharpest crowd inflections on the calendar. Districts across Florida, Georgia, Texas, and the Carolinas return in the first two weeks, and because those states supply a disproportionate share of Walt Disney World's drive-in and annual-pass attendance, Orlando empties out around the middle of the month while much of the rest of the country is still on vacation.

The stretch to avoid: the first two weeks at Walt Disney World, before Southern districts return.

If you have flexibility: the last ten days at Walt Disney World, the cheapest quiet stretch of the summer.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 92 degrees, lows around 75, about 17 days with measurable rain. Statistically indistinguishable from July, and in practice it feels worse because the overnight lows stop dropping and the ground never cools between days. Seventeen days with measurable rain, the same afternoon convective pattern, and the statistical ramp toward peak Atlantic hurricane season beginning mid-month.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 85 degrees, lows around 65, about 0 days with measurable rain. The hottest month of the Southern California year and still seven degrees cooler than Orlando with a fraction of the humidity. Inland Orange County does get occasional heat spells that push past 95 for several consecutive days, which is the one condition that makes Disneyland genuinely uncomfortable.

Cost. August sits in the moderate band. Late August is historically when Walt Disney World publishes its most aggressive room-only discounts and, in some cycles, dining offers, the resort is discounting into the annual attendance low that runs from late August through September, and the offers land before the low itself does. Date-based ticket tiers step down in the last third of the month, so the ticket, the room, and the airfare all move in the same direction within about ten days.

One thing worth knowing. The last ten days at Walt Disney World are the hidden bargain of the summer. The combination available in late August exists nowhere else on the calendar: low crowds, discounted rooms, lower ticket tiers, and summer operating hours that have not yet been shortened. September is quieter and cheaper still, but September has already lost the long evenings.

Who August suits. Late-August Walt Disney World trips, where crowds and prices both fall while everyone else is still at work.

Who it does not. The first two weeks at Walt Disney World, which combine peak heat with summer crowds.
