April at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for April is C minus. The most expensive and most crowded non-summer month, carrying the year's second-largest peak. Its saving grace is a genuine price and crowd cliff once the holiday clears.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. April runs high. April carries the residue of the staggered spring break calendar plus the single largest non-Christmas holiday peak of the year, and the two overlap. Easter falls in April in most years, and it pulls a two-week wave with it rather than a two-day one, because European and Latin American school holidays run on one side of the weekend and US districts run on the other.

The stretch to avoid: the nine days from Good Friday through the following Sunday, wherever the holiday falls.

If you have flexibility: the last full week of the month in a year when Easter falls early.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 84 degrees, lows around 62, about 6 days with measurable rain. April is the last comfortable month before the Florida summer pattern establishes itself, and the monthly average flatters the back half, late April regularly runs upper 80s with humidity climbing and the first afternoon thunderstorms appearing. The UV index reaches its annual maximum band this month, which catches people who packed for a number in the low 80s.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 73 degrees, lows around 53, about 3 days with measurable rain. The rain effectively stops in April and the marine layer that grays over May and June mornings is not yet a factor, which makes this the sunniest, most reliable month of the Southern California spring. Evenings still drop into the 50s.

Cost. April sits in the high band. Easter week is priced like Christmas week at Walt Disney World, the same top date-based ticket tier, the same peak hotel rate season, and it is the one spring period where discount room offers are simply not published for the dates at all. The mechanism worth understanding is the cliff on the far side.

One thing worth knowing. Work backward from the holiday before you pick a week. The single decision that determines whether an April trip is good or bad is which side of Easter it sits on. Count backward from the holiday and identify the Friday before; count forward and identify the Sunday after.

Who April suits. The last ten days in a year when Easter falls early.

Who it does not. The week either side of Easter, which prices and fills like Christmas week.
