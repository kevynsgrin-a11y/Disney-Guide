May at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for May is A minus. Book the first two weeks and this is close to the best trip available all year. Book the last week and you have paid summer prices for summer crowds without summer's long hours.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. May runs moderate. May splits at Memorial Day and the two halves have almost nothing in common. Everything before the holiday is the quietest stretch Walt Disney World sees between spring break and late August: schools are in session nationwide, the spring travel market has spent itself, and summer booking has not converted into arrivals yet.

The stretch to avoid: memorial Day weekend at both resorts, a three-day spike rather than a week.

If you have flexibility: the first two full weeks at Walt Disney World, the best non-fall window of the year.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 89 degrees, lows around 68, about 8 days with measurable rain. May is the pivot month: the first half still behaves like spring with tolerable humidity, and the second half behaves like summer once the afternoon convective pattern establishes itself. The monthly average of eight rain days hides that split, most of them fall in the back half.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 75 degrees, lows around 57, about 2 days with measurable rain. The numbers understate how gray May can be, because the marine layer holds overcast until early afternoon on a large share of days. That is unflattering in photographs and genuinely excellent for standing in an outdoor queue, which is a trade most visitors would take if they knew it was on offer.

Cost. May sits in the moderate band. Early May sits in one of the two lowest date-based ticket tiers of the year at Walt Disney World, and resort hotels hold spring shoulder rates right up until the Memorial Day season begins, which means the cheapest and the most expensive nights of the month can be nine days apart for the same room. Memorial Day weekend itself carries a holiday rate season and a top ticket tier.

One thing worth knowing. Check the operating hours before you build an evening plan. Magic Kingdom hosts private graduation events on a number of weeknights in May, and on those nights the park closes to regular guests several hours earlier than the published seasonal pattern would suggest. A touring plan that assumes a 10 PM close and finds a 7 PM close loses three of its best hours, the ones with the shortest waits and the coolest temperatures.

Who May suits. Anyone with date flexibility in the first half of the year.

Who it does not. Memorial Day weekend and the week after, when the summer pattern locks in early.
