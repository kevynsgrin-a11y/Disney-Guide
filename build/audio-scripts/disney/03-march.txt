March at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for March is C plus. Perfect Orlando weather, permanently high attendance, and upper-tier pricing for four straight weeks. Go in the first week or accept that every day will be busy.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. March runs high. US school districts break for spring across a five-to-six-week span and they do not coordinate with each other. Texas goes in the first half of the month, Florida's own districts go earlier than almost anyone, the Northeast clusters around the third week, and the Midwest scatters across all of it.

The stretch to avoid: the third and fourth weeks, when the largest Northeast districts overlap with everyone else.

If you have flexibility: the first full week at Walt Disney World, measurably the softest of the month.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 79 degrees, lows around 58, about 7 days with measurable rain. This is the best weather month of the Orlando year by a clear margin, warm afternoons, cool evenings, low humidity, and no afternoon convective storm pattern yet. It is also precisely why March is priced and attended the way it is.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 71 degrees, lows around 50, about 5 days with measurable rain. The rain season is winding down but is not finished, and mornings are colder than the daily high suggests, a park opening rope drop can be in the low 50s with the sun barely up. Afternoons are close to ideal.

Cost. March sits in the high band. Walt Disney World's date-based ticket tiers sit in their upper bands for essentially the whole month, and the resort hotels run a spring rate season that does not dip between individual districts' breaks, because there is no week when demand actually falls. Promotional room offers, when they exist at all in spring, exclude March dates.

One thing worth knowing. The trough that does exist is at the front of the month. The first full week of March is consistently the softest week at Walt Disney World. Florida and Texas districts have mostly not gone yet, northern districts are two to three weeks away, and the winter market has cleared.

Who March suits. Anyone whose priority is comfortable temperatures over short lines.

Who it does not. Anyone hoping to find a quiet week.
