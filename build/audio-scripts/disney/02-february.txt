February at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for February is B. Excellent everywhere except Presidents' week, which behaves like a holiday week. Four festivals run this month, more seasonal content per day than any other point on the calendar.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. February runs moderate. February is two quiet stretches separated by one hard spike, and the spike has a different mechanism from spring break. Most of the Northeast and Midwest take a mid-winter break in the same week around Presidents' Day, and unlike spring break, which staggers across five or six weeks by district, this one is synchronized.

The stretch to avoid: presidents' week, roughly the Saturday before the holiday through the following weekend.

If you have flexibility: the first full week, effectively an extension of late January.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 75 degrees, lows around 53, about 7 days with measurable rain. February is the driest stretch of the Orlando year with the lowest humidity, and the afternoons are the most comfortable the park schedule ever offers. Mornings and nights are cold by Florida standards, a 6 PM temperature in the upper 50s is normal, and the water parks close outright when a front pushes through.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 69 degrees, lows around 48, about 6 days with measurable rain. February is the wettest month of the Southern California year on the normals, and when a system stalls it rains for two days rather than twenty minutes. That is a planning risk and a planning opportunity in equal measure, because rain is the one condition that reliably thins a Disneyland crowd.

Cost. February sits in the moderate band. The pricing structure this month is unusually legible. Walt Disney World's date-based ticket tiers step up for Presidents' week and step straight back down after it, and the resort hotels carry a distinct Presidents' week rate season that sits well above the weeks on either side for the identical room.

One thing worth knowing. If your dates are Presidents' week, shift right rather than left. The asymmetry in this week is the useful part. Attendance ramps into Presidents' week over several days as districts release at slightly different times, but it clears in about two days afterward, because a synchronized break ends synchronously.

Who February suits. Food and festival trips.

Who it does not. Presidents' week, which prices and fills like Easter at both resorts.
