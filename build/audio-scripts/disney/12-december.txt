December at Walt Disney World and Disneyland Resort. Your month in about three minutes, from Ride Ready Guide.

Our grade for December is C. Early December is an A. The holiday week is close to an F.

Everything in this brief was checked in July 2026. Nothing here is a forecast for a specific date. These are multi-year patterns we plan against, not a promise.

Crowds. December runs peak. Through roughly the first two weeks, Walt Disney World runs at moderate levels with the entire holiday product already installed, the decorations, the castle lighting, the Festival of the Holidays, the party nights, the seasonal overlays. Then, over about a week, the curve goes vertical.

The stretch to avoid: christmas Eve through New Year's Day at both resorts, the worst stretch of the year without exception.

If you have flexibility: the first full week, the quietest week of the fourth quarter and the best value on the calendar.

Weather. Climate normals over a thirty-year reference period, not a forecast.

Walt Disney World: highs around 74 degrees, lows around 54, about 7 days with measurable rain. Alongside January this is the mildest month of the year with the lowest humidity, and the afternoons are close to ideal for a full park day. The variance is the problem: a cold front can hold highs in the 50s and drop overnight lows into the 30s for several days, and Orlando's parks have very little indoor waiting space when that happens.

Disneyland Resort: highs around 68 degrees, lows around 45, about 5 days with measurable rain. Cool and one of Southern California's wetter months. The resort's nighttime holiday shows run in genuinely cold conditions by local standards, and evening temperatures in the 40s regularly catch visitors who packed for California rather than for a December night outdoors.

Cost. December sits in the peak band. The lever in December is the room, not the ticket. Walt Disney World resort rates can approach double between the first week and the last week of the same month for an identical room, while the date-based ticket tiers move much less than that, which is exactly why the value case for early December is so strong and why late December is expensive in a way no strategy touches.

One thing worth knowing. If you have any choice at all, take the first two weeks. Everything that makes a December trip worth taking, the decorations, the castle lighting, the seasonal parades and shows, the Festival of the Holidays, the party nights, the overlays at both resorts, is fully installed and running by the start of the month. None of it improves in the final two weeks.

Who December suits. The first two weeks, which pair the full holiday installation with moderate crowds and shoulder rates.

Who it does not. The days around Christmas and New Year, categorically, at any budget and with any strategy.
