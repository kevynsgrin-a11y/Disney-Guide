# Halloween brand film credits and provenance

The five files in this directory are original media produced for Ride Ready Guide on 2026-09-09. Four scene keyframes were generated as visual anchors. Veo 3.1 created the corresponding silent eight-second 1080p motion sources. FFmpeg uniformly retimed each source to six seconds at 24 frames per second and concatenated the four final clips into an exact 24-second silent master. The intermediate keyframes, source videos, and concat manifest were removed after final delivery.

The visual package is an independent editorial-documentary treatment of a generic Halloween fairground. It includes only low unbranded fairground forms, ordinary string lights, generic triangle-face jack-o-lanterns, anonymous silhouettes, and generic homemade-costume forms. The ending uses a rear-facing child silhouette on a generic lamplit path as a thematic seasonal echo of the prior brand film’s quiet end-of-evening visual language. It does not use prior footage, a real venue, a recognizable person, or a copied park scene.

Every generation prompt explicitly prohibited operator characters, logos, wordmarks, branded ride vehicles, monorails, operator costuming, licensed character costumes, three-circle arrangements, castles, mansions, recognizable venues, recognizable architecture, text, watermarks, and identifiable faces. The party scene permits only anonymous, blurred or silhouetted generic homemade forms such as an unmarked witch hat, cardboard robot, sheet ghost, and ordinary coats. No music, natural ambience, dialogue, or other audio is present in the final clips.

The scene plan and delivery specifications are documented in `docs/HALLOWEEN-BRAND-FILM.md`.
