# Brand film credits and provenance

## `01-drop.mp4`, `02-lights.mp4`, `03-splash.mp4`, `04-day-end.mp4`, and `brand-film-24s.mp4`

- **Provenance:** original media generated for this repository on 2026-09-09 with Manus built-in image generation for scene keyframes, Veo 3.1 for the four motion sources, and Manus built-in music generation for the original instrumental score. FFmpeg retimed each 1080p source uniformly from eight to six seconds, preserved and mixed the embedded natural ambience, mixed in time-aligned score segments, and stitched the four delivery clips into the 24-second master.
- **Licence:** generated for Ride Ready Guide. No external image, video, music, or source reference material was supplied or incorporated.
- **Visual scope:** an original generic coaster scene, fairground lights and carousel, an original generic log-flume boat, and an anonymous parent-and-child silhouette. The work is editorial-documentary in treatment and does not depict a real park, real venue, operator branding, characters, costumed figures, monorails, recognizable faces, or recognizable architecture.
- **IP controls:** every generation prompt prohibited park-operator characters, logos, wordmarks, branded ride vehicles, monorails, costumed characters, three-circle arrangements, real park castles or recognizable venues, text, watermarks, and identifiable people. The original coaster train and log-flume boat are generic, unbranded forms rather than any operator's vehicle design.
- **Audio:** the delivery clips carry an original, subtle instrumental score under scene-specific natural ambience. There are no vocals, lyrics, spoken dialogue, readable visual titles, or on-screen text.
- **Documentation:** `docs/BRAND-FILM.md` holds the time-coded creative, sound, and assembly plan.
