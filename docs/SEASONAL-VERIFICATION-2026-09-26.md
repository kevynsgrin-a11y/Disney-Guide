This is the source decision record for the three sites in this repository. An event's first and last dates are a **season range**, never proof that a party or haunt operates on every intervening day. ## Search markup and rollover

Google advises separate `Event` items for distinct performances in its [Event structured data guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/event). This branch falls back to `Article` markup for select-night spans and uses `Event` only for confirmed continuous runs. An attraction included with **paid** park admission is not marked freely accessible to the public. Homepage Halloween plates carry a final date so the browser removes them after the season; a November build must also advance `BUILD_MONTH` in `src/lib/staleness.mjs` so pages remain correct for visitors without JavaScript and for search crawlers. The Disney month-data change requires regenerated committed audio scripts.

## Differentiation hypothesis

TouringPlans already offers [HHN dates](https://storage-cdn.touringplans.com/universal-orlando/events/halloween-horror-nights-orlando) and a [touring plan](https://touringplans.com/blog/guide-to-halloween-horror-nights-at-universal-orlando/); Undercover Tourist offers [early-entry guidance](https://www.undercovertourist.com/blog/halloween-horror-nights-early-entry-universal-orlando/); Orlando Informer offers [early admission](https://orlandoinformer.com/universal/halloween-horror-nights-early-admission) and [touring plan](https://orlandoinformer.com/universal/halloween-horror-nights-touring-plan/) pages. A cross-operator, source-stamped **day-to-night handoff card** remains a testable gap: choose park, exact date and party profile; see daytime event, closure/changeover, separate ticket or maze-pass requirements, sensory/age notes, and a printable offline checklist. Do not describe it as unique until a fuller competitor inventory confirms the gap. The current Hollywood Haunt Planner is a narrower first step: official 2026 house shortlist, visitor-entered ticket comparison and print checklist, with no claimed queue forecast.
