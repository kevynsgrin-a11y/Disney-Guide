# Brand film production blueprint

## Creative definition

This social film is an original, silent-title master for an independent theme-park planning publication. It progresses from **energy to tenderness** across four six-second scenes: a generic coaster crest, a fairground waking at night, an original log-flume splash, and a parent carrying a sleeping child away from a warm, indistinct fairground. The editorial-documentary register deliberately observes moments rather than selling them.

Every scene uses the same visual grammar: a full-frame 35 mm documentary perspective, warm amber practical highlights, cool blue or slate shadows, restrained contrast, and a light natural film-grain texture. There are no titles, superimposed graphics, logos, readable signs, or watermarks. All figures are anonymous, silhouetted, turned away, mid-distance, or obscured by motion and water.

## Four-scene plan

| Time | Clip | Narrative purpose | Camera and action | Sound and score state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00–00:06 | `01-drop` | Establish kinetic joy | A distant, unbranded coaster train crests a modest drop at dusk; a locked 3/4 rear-side camera follows only a slight natural vibration while the background blurs. | Wind, track rush, distant delighted voices without intelligible words; score rises from 4/10 to 6/10. |
| 00:06–00:12 | `02-lights` | Transition from speed to wonder | A locked wide shot looks through string-light bokeh as an unbranded carousel turns slowly within a generic night fairground. | Soft carousel mechanism, distant crowd wash, warm light ambience; score settles at 5/10. |
| 00:12–00:18 | `03-splash` | Brief human release | A generic log-flume boat meets the bottom of a chute, producing one backlit sheet of spray while riders remain anonymous and mid-distance. | One water impact and soft, unidentifiable laughter; score peaks gently at 6/10 and releases to 4/10. |
| 00:18–00:24 | `04-day-end` | Tender resolution | A parent carries a sleeping child away down a lamplit path, viewed from behind as the fairground becomes bokeh. | Footsteps and distant fairground wash; score thins from 3/10 to 1/10 with a gentle final sustain. |

## Sound identity

A single 24-second instrumental score is generated in D minor at 76 BPM. Its locked palette is warm felt piano, soft analog pad, delicate celesta accents, round sub-bass, and brushed low percussion. It contains no vocals. The score will be mixed under the clips’ natural ambience at a restrained level, preserving wind, carousel mechanism, water impact, footsteps, and non-intelligible distant crowd sound.

## IP and identity controls

The scenes depict only generic fairground forms and original, unbranded vehicles. No park-operator characters, logos, wordmarks, branded ride vehicles, monorails, costumed characters, real park castles, recognizable venues, recognizable faces, or three-circle arrangements are permitted. The final master remains title-free so any social copy can be added separately in an editorial environment.
