# Halloween brand film production blueprint

## Creative definition

This is a silent, original **24-second** social-film master for an independent theme-park planning publication. The film moves from **anticipation to warmth** through four six-second scenes: lights arriving at gloaming, intimate pumpkin detail, an abstract party of anonymous homemade-costume silhouettes, and a child’s quiet walk home. It has no titles, captions, superimposed graphics, music, ambience, dialogue, logos, or watermarks.

The shared visual grammar is editorial documentary cinema rather than seasonal advertising. Each scene uses warm amber practical highlights, deep plum shadows, restrained contrast, a full-frame perspective, and fine natural film grain. All figures are anonymous through distance, silhouette, back view, depth-of-field blur, or a combination of these techniques.

## Continuity controls

All four clips share the same color temperature, deep plum shadow density, restrained grain, and slow documentary pacing. A six-second delivery clip is made from each controlled eight-second 1080p source by uniform retiming. The clips remain separate, title-free assets while the joined master uses hard cuts at 00:06, 00:12, and 00:18 to retain the purposeful four-part editorial rhythm.

## Identity and IP controls

The scenes use only generic fairground forms. The only pumpkin carvings allowed are ordinary triangle eyes, triangle noses, and small zigzag mouths. The party may contain only homemade generic silhouettes such as an unmarked witch hat, cardboard robot, sheet ghost, and ordinary coats. No licensed character costume, operator costume, mascot, branded prop, logo, wordmark, ride vehicle, monorail, recognizable venue, castle, mansion, recognizable architecture, three-circle arrangement, text, watermark, or identifiable face is permitted.

## Intended delivery

The package contains four silent, 1920 × 1080 H.264 MP4 clips, each exactly 6.0 seconds at 24 fps, and one silent stitched 24.0-second master. It is a social-media asset package and does not alter the website’s existing hero behavior or current live seasonal content.
